About

Hi, I'm Naomi. I make science visuals

I’m a medical illustrator from Puerto Rico, with a passion for mental health, diversity, and making science accessible to all audiences. I create visuals that spark understanding, empathy, and curiosity, with the audience always in mind. Whether I’m prototyping a digital tool or illustrating an educational resource, I care deeply about clarity, inclusivity, accessibility, and impact. I’m always learning, and always looking for ways to help people see the world a little differently.
